## Possible projects

### Math-Harness for Small reasoning languages. 
It is clear that advanced models, such Astra or Fable, are very good at solving math problems, and that the smaller models do not match their performance right-away. Is it possible to improve the math-research capacities of a small reasoning model with a good harness? How can we measure such progress?

### Algebraic version of Topological Data Analysis (TDA)

TDA is an important tool that captures the topological complexity of a given dataset. Can one develop a parallel theory that captures its algebraic complexity? I was thinking one can consider the polynomials of degree < d that almost vanish on the data, that is p(x) < \epsilon for each x in dataset and with |p| =1. The ideal generated by  such polynomials carry invariants, such as the betti numbers of their resolution, and they may capture information about the initial data. As d and \epsilon vary, these invariants form a two-parameter family, much like a barcode, and an initial question is whether they are stable under small perturbations of the data.
